Your homie for disciplined trading.

TradeHomie floats over your TradingView chart and asks the questions you'd ask yourself on a good day — before you click buy or sell, not after.

What it does

Four small habits, built into the chart.

Pre-trade checklist

Your own Entry Rules and Risk Management, plus a locked FOMO/Psychology list you can't quietly edit away.

Position-size calculator

Capital, risk % and live FX rates, worked out for you before you size a trade.

Private local journal

Chart screenshots stay on your device only — never uploaded, ever.

A daily mood check

A quick, awareness-only check-in on how you're feeling before you trade.

TradeHomie doesn't give financial advice. It helps you stay aware of your own process. Nothing in the extension tells you what to buy, sell, or when — that call is always yours.
