Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

This policy explains what TradeHomie ("we", "us") collects when you use the TradeHomie Chrome extension, and — just as importantly — what we deliberately never collect.

Screenshots never leave your device

Chart screenshots you capture inside TradeHomie are stored only in your browser's local storage, on your own device. We never upload them, never see them, and they never touch our servers. If you use TradeHomie on a different computer, screenshots from your other device won't be there — that's intentional, not a bug.

What we do store

To run the checklist, journal, and subscription features, the following is stored in our database, protected so that only you can read your own data:

  • Your email address, used only to sign you in with a one-time code — no password.
  • Your custom checklist rules (Entry Rules, Risk Management).
  • Your journal entries: the symbol and direction you logged, your strategy notes, which checklist rules were checked at the time, any override declaration or skip reason, your calculator inputs, and any outcome you record (win/loss/breakeven/partial, notes, P&L).
  • A reference id linking a journal entry to its screenshots on your device — just a pointer, never the image itself.
  • Your trial and subscription status, computed and controlled only by our server — the extension itself can never change this.

Payments

Payments are handled entirely by Dodo Payments, our payment processor. We never see, store, or process your card details ourselves.

Email delivery

Sign-in codes are sent through Brevo, our email delivery provider. Brevo sees your email address and the code itself — nothing else about your account or trading activity.

What we don't do

  • No advertising trackers, no analytics scripts, no third-party tracking of any kind inside the extension.
  • We don't sell or share your data with anyone for marketing purposes.
  • We don't give financial advice, and we don't use your data to generate trading signals.

Your data, your control

You can ask us to delete your account and all associated data at any time by emailing tradehomie.creator@gmail.com. We'll confirm once it's done.

Age

TradeHomie is intended for users who are at least 18 years old.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Continuing to use TradeHomie after a change means you accept the update.

Contact

Questions about this policy: tradehomie.creator@gmail.com
